How we work

A Clear Path From Enquiry to Follow-Through.

Six steps that keep you informed and in control of what is agreed.

Start Your Request

The process may vary according to the service requested. Sending a request does not book or confirm a service.

  1. Submit your requirements

    You send an enquiry through the website, email or WhatsApp.

  2. Requirements assessment

    We review the service type, location, scope, timelines and other relevant information.

  3. Provider sourcing and quotation

    Where required, we identify suitable service providers, obtain quotations and assess the proposed arrangements.

  4. Customer review and approval

    You review the proposed scope, pricing, timeline and terms before accepting the quotation.

  5. Service confirmation

    Once the arrangements and payment terms are agreed, Movelink confirms the service.

  6. Coordination and follow-up

    We coordinate the agreed arrangements, monitor progress and share relevant updates with you.
