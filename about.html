Company overview

One Team Coordinating the Moving Parts.

MOVELINK INTEGRATED LOGISTICS SOLUTIONS LIMITED is a Nigerian logistics coordination, supply chain management, procurement, transportation coordination and industrial project support company, based in Lagos.

We serve small and medium-sized businesses, larger companies and industrial organisations that want a dependable partner to organise logistics without building an in-house team.

A Partnership-Driven, Asset-Light Approach

How we operate

Movelink works with third-party service providers rather than relying on company-owned assets. This lets us look for the provider that fits each requirement, location and budget.

How we coordinate

We take your requirements, source and compare providers and quotations, review the proposal with you, and then coordinate the agreed arrangements and follow up on progress. You keep one point of contact throughout.

Our mission

To give businesses dependable, well-coordinated logistics and supply chain support, so they can focus on their core work.

Our vision

To be a trusted logistics coordination partner for businesses across Nigeria.
