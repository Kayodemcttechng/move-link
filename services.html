Movelink is a coordination partner. We work with third-party service providers rather than relying on company-owned assets, and we do not offer freight forwarding, customs clearance or shipping services.

Transportation and Distribution Coordination

Movelink coordinates suitable transport arrangements, distribution requirements and delivery schedules through appropriate service providers, so you deal with one point of contact instead of many.

Customer needs it addresses

Moving goods between locations on a schedule, and keeping deliveries organised across multiple drops.

How Movelink supports you

Reviewing the route and delivery requirements, sourcing and comparing provider quotations, and following up on the agreed schedule.
